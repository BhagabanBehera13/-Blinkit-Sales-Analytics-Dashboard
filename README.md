🛒 Blinkit Sales Analytics Dashboard
Overview

The Blinkit Sales Analytics Dashboard is an interactive Business Intelligence project developed using Power BI to analyze Blinkit's retail sales data and generate meaningful insights into outlet performance, item categories, fat content, and outlet establishment trends.

The project transforms raw retail sales data into interactive visualizations and dashboards that help understand sales performance, product mix, and outlet characteristics across Blinkit's outlet network.

The analysis covers the complete data analytics workflow, including data preparation, exploratory analysis, visualization, dashboard development, and business insight generation.

🎯 Business Objectives

The primary objectives of this Blinkit Sales Analytics project are:

Analyze overall sales performance across all outlets.
Monitor total sales, average sales, number of items, and average rating.
Analyze sales distribution by fat content (Low Fat vs Regular).
Compare fat content distribution across outlet tiers.
Analyze sales performance across different item types.
Understand outlet establishment trends over the years.
Analyze outlet distribution by size (Small, Medium, High).
Compare outlet performance across locations (Tier 1, Tier 2, Tier 3).
Analyze sales, ratings, and item visibility across outlet types.
Support retail decision-making through interactive dashboards.
